## Research Focus

Optimization theory and test-time compute for reasoning-oriented large language models — post-training methods, preference optimization, alignment, and verifier/PRM-guided search across autoregressive and diffusion language models. A parallel line of work designs evolving, self-organizing multi-agent systems with dynamic reputation and trust modelling, and develops reinforcement learning methods for reasoning and non-stationary decision problems, with applications extending to diffusion-based generative modeling for wireless and signal-processing systems.

## Current Work

- **Adaptive test-time compute for LLM reasoning** *(REAL Lab, OU)* — verifier/PRM-guided reasoning, agentic RL controllers, trajectory-level search across autoregressive (ICML'26) and diffusion (COLM'26) language models.
- **Diffusion-based wireless estimation & neural Gaussian radio fields** *(Stanford AI Lab)* — verifier-guided trajectory search for diffusion test-time scaling in channel prediction (KDD'26).
- **Internet of Agents platform** *(Conoid.ai)* — a decentralized, self-evolving multi-agent system built on trust/memory-based team formation and a secure HTTP agent-communication protocol.
- **Meta-thinking in LLM agents** *(survey, independent)* — a survey of meta-thinking via multi-agent reinforcement learning (IEEE TAI, under review).
