## 👋 Who I Am

I'm a **Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Oklahoma** (REAL Lab), advised by **[Dr. Dean Hougen](https://www.cs.ou.edu/~hougen/resume.html)**, and a **Research Collaborator at the Stanford AI Lab (SAIL)** working with **Prof. John M. Cioffi** and **Dr. Emily Fox**. My work sits at the intersection of **LLM reasoning, test-time compute, and agentic AI** — with a parallel thread applying generative modeling (diffusion) to wireless/signal systems.

Before my Ph.D. I earned a **B.E. in Electrical Engineering from NUST-SEECS** (CGPA 3.66, Specialization GPA 4.0) and worked as a **Machine Learning Engineer at Cowlar Design Studio (YC '21)**, shipping computer-vision systems to production for retail and manufacturing clients.

- 🎓 Ph.D. Computer Science, University of Oklahoma — 2024–Present
- 🧪 Graduate Researcher, REAL Lab (OU) & Research Collaborator, Stanford AI Lab
- 📝 22 publications across ICML, NeurIPS, COLM, KDD, AAAI, EMNLP, ICASSP, ICC, TMLR
- 🏅 Gallogly College of Engineering Graduate Fellow (2025)

## 🚧 Current Work

| Project | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Adaptive test-time compute for LLM reasoning** | REAL Lab, OU | Verifier/PRM-guided reasoning, agentic RL controllers, and trajectory-level search across autoregressive (`ICML'26`) and diffusion (`COLM'26`) language models |
| **Diffusion-based wireless estimation & nGRF** | Stanford AI Lab | Neural Gaussian Radio Fields + verifier-guided trajectory search for diffusion test-time scaling in channel prediction (`KDD'26`) |
| **Internet of Agents Platform** | Conoid.ai | Decentralized, self-evolving multi-agent platform — trust/memory-based team formation, HTTP agent-communication protocol, security-by-design |
| **Meta-thinking survey for LLM agents** | Independent | Survey on meta-thinking via multi-agent reinforcement learning (`EAAI`, submitted) |
